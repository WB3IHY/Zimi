libzim-wasm.js and libzim-wasm.wasm
===================================

The release build of javascript-libzim v0.95 (published 2026-08-06), shipped
unmodified, the WORKERFS variant. It is libzim compiled to WebAssembly with
Xapian, which gives Zimi's "Keep on this phone" its full-text search over a ZIM
kept in the browser's private storage. It runs in a Web Worker the page starts
on first use (zimi/static/keepstore.js); the service worker never loads it.
