Royal Game of Ur

Finkel's rules. Race all 7 pieces along your path and off the board with an exact throw. Four dice give 0 to 4. Landing on an enemy piece in the middle lane sends it back to the start. The central rosette is safe. Any rosette gives another throw. Tap a piece that can move (or its target square) to play it. Arrow keys move, Enter plays, R or Space rolls, H hints, N starts a new game.
