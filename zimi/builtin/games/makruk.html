Makruk

Thai chess. The Khun is the king; the Met steps one square on a diagonal; the Khon steps on a diagonal or straight ahead; the Ma jumps; the Rua slides; the Bia moves one square and takes on a forward diagonal, and becomes a Met on the sixth rank. No castling, no double step. Sixty-four moves each without a capture or a Bia move is a draw. Arrow keys move, Enter selects, U undoes, H hints, F flips, N starts a new game.
