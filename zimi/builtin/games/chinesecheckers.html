Chinese Checkers

Move a marble to a next-door hole, or jump over any marble into the hole beyond it, and keep jumping in a chain. Be first to fill the triangle across the star. Tap a marble, then a hole. With the keyboard, arrow keys move the cursor, Enter selects, U undoes, N starts a new game.
