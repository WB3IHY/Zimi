Hnefatafl

Copenhagen rules. The attackers (dark) move first and try to capture the king. The defenders (light) try to take the king to a corner. Pieces move in straight lines like rooks. A piece is captured when it is between two enemy pieces. Corners and the empty throne count as enemies, and only the king may stop there. The king is captured by 4 attackers, or by 3 attackers and the throne. Arrow keys move, Enter selects, U undoes, H hints, N starts a new game.
