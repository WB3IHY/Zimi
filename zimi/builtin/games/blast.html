W A S D (or the arrows with one person) walk, Space or Enter drops a bomb. With two people, player 2 uses the arrow keys and Enter. A bomb blasts in four lines and sets off other bombs it reaches; stone stops it, soft blocks break and sometimes hide a power-up: more flame, an extra bomb, or speed. You can step off your own bomb, but not back on. P pauses.
