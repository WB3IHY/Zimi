Mahjong for Four

Hong Kong old style, simplified: no flowers, a hand needs three faan (see Scoring) and may be won on your own draw or on a discard. Tap a tile to pick it, tap it again to discard. When another player discards you may claim it for a pung or kong (any player), a chow (only the player after them) or to win. East round: the deal passes on when someone else wins. Arrow keys move over your tiles, Enter picks and discards, H hints, N starts a new game.
