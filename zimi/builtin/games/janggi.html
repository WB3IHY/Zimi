Janggi

Cho (green) moves first. Chariots and cannons also follow the palace diagonals, a cannon must jump exactly one piece and never a cannon, elephants step one straight and two across. If the generals face each other with nothing between them (bikjang) the game is drawn. Arrow keys move, Enter selects, U undoes, P passes, H hints, F flips, N starts a new game.
