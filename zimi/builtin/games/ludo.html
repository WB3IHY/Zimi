Ludo

Roll a six to bring a token out (with nothing on the board you get three tries), and roll again after every six (three sixes in a row lose the turn). Land alone on a rival to send it back to its yard, except on the coloured start squares and the stars, which are safe. Reach the middle with an exact roll. Tab picks a token, Enter moves it, R rolls, 1 to 4 pick a token, N starts a new game.
