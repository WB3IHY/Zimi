Carrom

Aim with sliders

Pocket your coins (white or black) with the striker; each of your own coins you pocket earns another shot. The red queen must be covered by pocketing one of your own coins right after, or she goes back to the middle. The striker in a pocket is a foul. The first side to clear all nine coins wins. On a touch screen, flick the striker (slide along the baseline to place it); with a mouse, drag back from the striker to aim and set the power, then let go. Keys: arrows place, A and D aim, W and S power, Enter shoots.
