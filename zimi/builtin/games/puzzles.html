Puzzles

41 logic puzzles, played offline. Each one makes a new game on demand and can show its solution when you are stuck.

Number puzzles

SoloSudoku, with jigsaw, X and killer variants KeenFill the grid. Each cage must make its number. TowersPlace towers. Clues say how many you can see. UnequalFill the grid. Follow the greater-than and less-than signs. GroupUnfinished upstream. Fill a grid from a group table. FillingFill in numbers so each region's size matches its number RangeShade squares. Each number shows how many squares it can see. SinglesShade repeated numbers. Shaded squares may not touch. UnrulyUse two colours. No three in a row. Each line is balanced.

Logic grids

TentsPitch a tent beside every tree. No tents may touch. MagnetsPlace magnets so every line has the right poles DominosaTile the grid so every number pair is used once MapColour the map with four colours. Neighbours must differ.

Drawing lines

BridgesJoin islands with bridges. Each number says how many. LoopyDraw one loop. Each number counts the sides used around its cell. PearlDraw a loop through the pearls as shown SlantDraw diagonal lines. Each number shows how many lines touch it. SignpostLink the squares in order by following the arrows. TracksLay a railway from one end to the other UntangleDrag the points until no lines cross

Regions and shapes
