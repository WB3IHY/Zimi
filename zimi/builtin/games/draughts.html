Draughts 10x10

Ten by ten, twenty men each. Men step forward on a diagonal and capture forwards or backwards; kings fly any distance. You must capture, and you must take the sequence that captures the most pieces. Tap a piece, then where it lands (for a chain of captures, tap each landing). Arrow keys move, Enter selects, U undoes, H hints, F flips, N starts a new game.
